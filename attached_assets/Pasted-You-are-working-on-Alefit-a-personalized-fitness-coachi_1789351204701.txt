You are working on Alefit, a personalized fitness coaching app.

Your job is NOT to create a generic fitness website.

Your job is to transform the existing Alefit project into a polished, premium, highly personalized fitness app that feels competitive with successful modern fitness products.

The main inspiration for product clarity and structure is apps such as Runna, but DO NOT copy Runna’s branding, exact layouts, wording, illustrations, or proprietary design.

Alefit must have its own identity.

The core product idea is:

Alefit learns about the user, creates a training plan specifically for them, tells them exactly what to do today, tracks their progress, and adapts their future training.

The personalized plan should be the center of the entire product.
