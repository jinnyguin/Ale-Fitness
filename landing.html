Built around you

Your context belongs in your plan.

Sport + schedule

Practices, games, races, and real availability count as workload. Training is placed around them instead of pretending they do not exist.

Readiness + recovery

Energy, soreness, time, and extra activity can change today’s dose while preserving the plan’s longer-term direction.

Evidence over promises

Progress is your logged work and what you learn from it—not a projection, guarantee, or transformation claim.
