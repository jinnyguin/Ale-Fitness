## Brand Guidelines

- **Colors:** #0F0F0F (dark background), #D4FF00 (lime accent), white text
- **Font:** Barlow Condensed for headlines, Inter for body text
- **Logo:** Use images/alefit-logo.png (transparent PNG)
- **Call to action:** Action-oriented: "Start Training", "Get Your Plan", "Sign Up Free"
- **Image size:** 1080x1080 for Instagram feed, 1080x1920 for Instagram Stories/Reels
